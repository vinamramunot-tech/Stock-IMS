Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mava Gems ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile and desktop inventory application (the "App").

Summary: Mava Gems is designed with a privacy-first, offline architecture. We do not collect, track, store on remote servers, or sell your personal data or inventory records. All data remains exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

The App requests minimal device permissions strictly required to provide jewelry stock management functionality:

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to allow you to capture photos of your jewelry pieces and gemstones to attach to item records. Photos are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used to allow you to select existing jewelry images from your gallery to include in inventory entries, or to save exported catalog cards and tags to your photo library.
  • Local Storage: Your jewelry database (including items, weights, rates, stone specifications, and pricing) is stored locally on your device in an AES-256 encrypted file vault.

2. Data Collection and Tracking

  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash-tracking networks, or tracking cookies.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account with us to use the App.
  • Zero Server Storage: We do not operate remote servers that collect or monitor your inventory data, revenue, or business operations.

3. Data Security and Encryption

Your local database files are protected using industry-standard AES-256 encryption with a unique, cryptographically random initialization vector (IV) generated for each save operation. Because your data is stored locally, you maintain complete ownership and control over your files.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user information or business data with third parties under any circumstances.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for business and commercial inventory management and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
